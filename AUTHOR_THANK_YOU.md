# Thank you to the challenge community

Thank you to the ICML 2026 Agent Reproduction Challenge organizers, the
maintainers of its public Spaces, judge, and verdict dataset, and the paper
authors who share code, data, and experimental details.

This repository is a coordination layer for that community. It exists to make
paper selection, compute constraints, evidence standards, and links between
independent reproductions easier to inspect.

Because this is not a paper repository, this note is addressed to the
challenge community rather than attributed to a particular paper's authors.
Each paper repository contains its own author-specific note.
